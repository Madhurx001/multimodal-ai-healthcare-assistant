# Literature Survey

A literature survey of 20 recent papers related to:
- multimodal healthcare AI,
- medical-image analysis,
- clinical-data fusion,
- explainable AI,
- Grad-CAM,
- SHAP,
- and healthcare decision support

has been completed.

## Base Paper
**Holistic AI in Medicine; Improved Performance and Explainability (xHAIM)**

## Main Findings
- Multimodal models frequently outperform comparable single-modality approaches.
- Explainability remains an important challenge in medical AI.
- Different modalities often require different explanation techniques.
- Real-world deployment requires attention to computation, missing modalities, external validation, and interpretability.

The detailed 20-paper comparison table from the Phase-I report can be added to this folder as a PDF, CSV, or Markdown table.
